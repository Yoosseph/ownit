# Code of Conduct

Ownit follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, give and take feedback gracefully, and
focus on what is best for the project and its users. Harassment, insults, personal attacks and
publishing other people's private information are not accepted, in issues, pull requests or
anywhere else the project lives.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior worries you, contact the maintainer, [@Yoosseph](https://github.com/Yoosseph),
privately. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, close threads, or
block people who break these rules.
